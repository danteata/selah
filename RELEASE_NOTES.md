## Selah 0.1.24

A fix for the projector, and for service orders that were not being saved. Please update before Sunday.

### The projector shows your slides again

- **In 0.1.23 the projector window stayed black.** Whatever was live, the output window showed nothing, on the desktop app and on the web, while the Program Output monitor on the operator's screen looked normal. Slides now show on the projector as they should. If you updated to 0.1.23, please update again.

### Your service order is saved

- **A service order prepared ahead of time is now saved.** Until now, slides added outside a live session lived only in that window: closing Selah, a restart, a crash or opening the schedule on another computer lost the whole order. It is now saved with the schedule and comes back when you open it.
- **Songs from your library can be saved and shared.** A song slide from your church's song library was refused when the schedule was saved, so services with songs never reached the other computers in a live session either. They now save and sync like any other slide.

### Smaller things

- **Song and hymn verses keep their line breaks.** Every verse used to run together as one long sentence, on the projector too. Each sung line now starts on its own line.
- **A video background you upload plays on the projector.** A video chosen as the background of a countdown, alert or slide only worked in the window you chose it in, and was lost after a reload. It is now kept on this computer and plays in the projector window.
- **Next Up no longer counts a countdown down early.** A countdown waiting its turn now shows its full time until it goes live.
- **The countdown panel fits a narrow sidebar.** The hours, minutes and seconds boxes no longer run off the edge.
- **When a save fails, Selah says so plainly:** "Couldn't save the service order".

### Worth knowing

- **Service orders are saved to Selah's servers**, as schedules already were. Media you upload stays on your computer and is not uploaded.
