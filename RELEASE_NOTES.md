## Selah 0.1.46

Arrange the desk the way you're used to: Studio and Dashboard are now one desk of panels you can move, stack and resize, with a Classic layout for anyone coming from EasyWorship. Song following and the Sermon Listener make far fewer wrong calls.

### The desk

- **Panels you arrange.** Service order, Preview, Live, the Sermon Listener and each library tool (Bible, Songs & Hymns, Media, Dictionary, Templates, Countdown, Alerts) are panels of their own. Drag the splitters between them to resize. Choose **Layout → Edit layout** to move them: drag a tab to the edge of another panel to split beside it, or onto a tab strip to stack with it. Done puts the desk back to normal use.
- **Classic layout.** Service order, Preview and Live across the top, and the library with the Sermon Listener along the bottom, as in EasyWorship. Pick it from the Layout menu.
- **Your own layouts.** **Save as…** keeps any arrangement under a name, and the Layout menu switches between them in one step. The menu marks a layout "modified" once you change it. Layouts are kept on this computer.
- **Preview.** Shows the slide you've staged in the service order, with Send, or what's next up when nothing is staged. Enter sends the staged slide from anywhere on the desk.
- **Lower thirds from the service order.** The service order's Add row now has Lower third beside Text slide; it replaces the Quick Actions sidebar, which is gone.
- The words on screen match: the slide queue is the **Service order**, and Live says **On air**.
- ⌘/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows) opens the command bar.
- Prefer the old Studio for now? **Settings → Display → Use classic workspace** brings it back for this release.

### Songs

- **A song you step away from stays away.** Putting a verse or announcement up while the congregation is still singing no longer gets taken back by the song a moment later; the song returns once the singing stops and starts again.
- **Preaching isn't mistaken for singing.** A sermon after a song, even one quoting it, no longer reads as the song still being sung, so verses are detected again straight away.
- **The right song, at the right place.** A detected song opens on the section being sung, not its first slide, and a wrong song is replaced once the congregation is clearly singing another.
- The first lines sung after clearing the transcript are no longer missed.

### Sermon Listener

- **Dismissing an error keeps the transcript.** Closing a notice such as "microphone reconnecting" mid-sermon no longer throws away what was heard.
- **A verse mentioned in passing follows auto-display.** Only a verse asked for ("turn to", "open", "go to") goes up regardless; "John 3:16 says…" with auto-display off doesn't. A chapter mentioned in passing no longer jumps the slide back to verse 1.
- **"The Lord is my shepherd"** is shown, as Psalms 23:1.
- **Speech after "read it in the NIV"** is detected again.
- A microphone that went missing, or a missing transcription model, now says so, and the model message tells you to download it in Settings.
- Restarting after a dropped microphone stays part of the same session: no extra chimes, and the free-plan time limit isn't reset.
- The team's saved copy of a sermon is saved however listening stops, once per session, even when the panel isn't open.
