## Selah 0.1.43

Phones on the church Wi-Fi now run the live session with the desk directly, internet or not. Selah also carries on quietly offline when its online service is unavailable, and asks that service for far less.

### Phones and the desk, over Wi-Fi

- **The phone app follows and drives the desk over Wi-Fi.** The live slide, the deck and suggestions go straight between the desk and the phones in the room, with no internet needed. When the internet is there, everything still reaches the online session too, so phones elsewhere keep following.
- **Pair a phone once.** On the desk, open **Phones** in the top bar and choose **Pair a phone**: it shows a six-digit code for two minutes. In the Android app, tap the **Desk** chip on the Live view (or More → On this Wi-Fi), then **Pair**, and enter the code. After that the phone connects on its own whenever it's on the same Wi-Fi as the desk.
- **Who can change slides:** a phone signed in as you, or as a church admin, runs the screen. Other phones follow the session's mode (Strict, Review or Open). Without an online session, set the mode in the desk's Phones panel.
- **The desk now always shows up.** It only announced itself once it had reached the online service, so with that down, phones found no desk.
- **Desk not found?** Check that Windows Firewall allows Selah (Windows Security → Firewall & network protection → Allow an app through firewall). Some church networks keep devices apart; if discovery is blocked but connections aren't, the Phones panel shows the desk's address, and the phone can connect by typing it.
- iPhones and phones using Selah in a browser keep using the online session as before.

### Working offline

- **No more "Selah hit an unexpected error" when the online service is unavailable.** Selah switches to offline mode and carries on with what's on this computer. It comes back by itself when the service does.
- **Templates stay listed offline.** Going offline used to empty the list of the church's templates.

### Lighter on the online service

- **The song library downloads only what changed**, not every song and its lyrics on each refresh.
- **Saved transcripts list as summaries.** The sermon in progress saves every five minutes and when listening stops, not every 15 seconds.
- **Phones and browsers fetch a template's background when it's shown**, rather than downloading all of them up front. The desk app still keeps them ready offline.
- Template thumbnails are smaller, connection checks happen only when the connection drops, and "who's here" updates are less frequent outside a live session.
