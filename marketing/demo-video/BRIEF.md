## Intent

The demo behind "Watch it work" on the Selah landing page. It follows one
Sunday service from the countdown before it starts to the last verse, and
shows each feature where a church actually uses it: the countdown on the
projector, worship lyrics, the pastor saying "turn to John 3:16" and the
verse reaching the screen before he has found it, and a second volunteer
helping from their phone. The goal, in the user's words: wow potential
customers with our awesome features.

## Assets

- Screens captured from the running app on this machine for a fictional
  sample church, seeded for this video. The user signs in once in Chrome;
  the projector output (/#/live) needs no sign-in.

## Customizations

- Show-it-as-is: the app's own captured screens, never rebuilt UI.
- Live words: the pastor's words type out as a live transcript, "John 3:16"
  lights up the moment it is recognised, then the verse lands on the
  projector. The product's signature moment ("Caught it the moment you said
  it" on the landing page).
- Silent: no narration, no music. On-screen type carries the story.
- Brand from the landing page: near-black #08090c, teal #14b8a6 / #0d9488,
  mint-to-gold gradient #5eead4 to #fcd34d, Crimson Pro (display, weight 600,
  sentence case), DM Sans (text).

## Notes

- Fictional church and names throughout. No invented statistics.
- "No internet" is claimed only for the desktop app: the web version's
  speech recognition needs a connection.
- No em dashes in on-screen text. Sentence case.
